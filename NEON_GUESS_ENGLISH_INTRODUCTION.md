### Quick Introduction

You can play it casually with friends or competitively in different multiplayer formats. The game is designed to be quick to understand, easy to join from a browser, and more exciting when everyone is playing together in real time.

## What Makes NEON GUESS Interesting?

NEON GUESS combines a familiar guessing-game concept with online multiplayer competition. Every player can join from a separate device, and the room state is synchronized in real time. This makes the experience more social than playing against a computer or using a traditional physical board.

The game is also flexible. The same core idea can support different categories and different match formats without changing the basic rules. Players can choose the format that fits the group: a private duel for two people, a team battle for four people, or a four-player competitive match where everyone takes part in the same contest.

The game is intentionally easy to explain: **create a room, invite the players, choose a category, observe the board, and guess the target.**

## Game Mode 1: 1v1 Guess Who

### Simple Explanation

**1v1 Guess Who is a private duel between two players. Each player receives a target, and both players compete to identify the other side’s target. The first player to make the correct guess has the advantage and can win the round.**

### How to Present It

**The 1v1 mode is the best mode for a quick head-to-head match. Two players create or join the same room, choose a category, and compete directly against each other. It is simple, fast, and very competitive because every question and every guess matters.**

### Best For

This mode is best for two friends who want a short challenge, a rematch, or a direct test of memory and deduction.

### Ready-to-Say Version

> **Let’s start with 1v1. It is a direct duel between two players. We choose a category, get our targets, and try to identify each other’s target before the other player does.**

## Game Mode 2: 2v2 Team Battle

### Simple Explanation

**2v2 Team Battle is a team-based mode for four players. Two teams compete against each other, and the teammates work together while trying to identify the opposing team’s target.**

### How to Present It

**The 2v2 mode adds teamwork to the guessing challenge. Instead of playing alone, you coordinate with your teammate, compare observations, and make better decisions together. It is more social than 1v1 because communication and cooperation become part of the strategy.**

### Best For

This mode is ideal when four friends want to cooperate, compete as teams, and communicate during the match.

### Ready-to-Say Version

> **If there are four of us, we can play 2v2 Team Battle. We split into two teams, communicate with our teammate, and try to outthink the other team. It is a mix of deduction, teamwork, and competition.**

## Game Mode 3: Four-Player Competitive Mode

### Simple Explanation

**The Four mode is designed for four players competing in the same match. Each player takes part in a larger competitive format, where observation, timing, and correct guesses determine who progresses and who wins.**

### How to Present It

**The Four mode is the best choice when everyone wants to compete individually in one shared match. It creates more pressure because there are more possible targets and more players to watch. The match can include multiple stages, so players need to stay focused from the beginning to the final result.**

### Best For

This mode is best for a group of four friends who want a more complete competitive experience instead of a simple duel or team match.

### Ready-to-Say Version

> **The Four mode is for four players competing in the same match. It feels more like a mini-tournament: everyone has to pay attention, make accurate guesses, and keep performing as the match progresses.**

## Categories and Content

Players can choose the type of content used in the match. Current examples include:

| Category | What Players Try to Identify |
|---|---|
| Cartoon Characters | A character from the available cartoon-character board |
| Football Players | A football player from the available player board |
| Types of Sports | A sport from the available sports board |

The category changes the knowledge and visual recognition challenge, while the game mode changes the way players compete. For example, the same football-player category can be used in 1v1, 2v2, or Four mode.

Future categories could include animals, jobs, food, vehicles, famous places, or other entertaining themes. This makes the game expandable without needing to create a completely new mode every time new content is added.

## How a Typical Game Works

1. **Create or join a private room.** One player creates a room and shares the invite code.
2. **Choose the category.** The group selects the type of characters, players, or sports to use.
3. **Wait for the players.** The room shows who has joined and whether the match is ready.
4. **Start the match.** The game assigns the targets and begins the round.
5. **Observe and think.** Players study the available options and use deduction rather than guessing blindly.
6. **Make a guess.** The player or team selects the target they believe is correct.
7. **Continue through the match.** Depending on the mode, the game proceeds through rounds or competitive stages until the winner is determined.
